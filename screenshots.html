Two screens working together

See the Game in Action

Each step pairs the private Host Console with the public Player Display, so you can see exactly what the host controls and what the room experiences.

Section 1

Getting Ready

The players enjoy the welcome screen while the host privately prepares categories and teams.

Step 1

Choose tonight’s categories

The host selects six topics and privately previews the available questions. The room sees only the welcoming farm scene.

Step 2

Get every team ready

The host confirms the active animal teams and explains the team card. The welcome screen remains undisturbed until Start Tonight is clicked.

Section 2

Playing a Question

The board, clue, answering team, result, and score stay synchronized while private controls remain with the host.

Step 3

Start the game board

The host operates the complete board and roster. Players see a clean large-format board with five animal teams per row.

Step 4

Show the clue

The host sees the question and private controls. Players see the clue in large type and the reminder to hold up their team card.

Step 5

Call one team

The host clicks the first team card spotted. Both screens identify the answering team, and only that team gives one answer.

Step 6

Score, reveal, and pass

The host marks the one answer. Players see the result, revealed answer, updated score, and reminder to pass the team card.

Section 3

Special Events & Final Challenge

The host controls the timing and private decisions while the room gets the full game-show reveal.

Step 7

Preview the Hoot & Howl

The host privately reviews or skips challenges. Players see the selected team waiting, without seeing the challenge early.

Step 8

Present the challenge

The host presents the chosen challenge and receives the private decision controls. The room sees the team, challenge, and point value.

Step 9

Find the Golden Horseshoe

The private console tracks the Boondoggle. Players see the leading team choose among three numbered hay bales.

Step 10

Begin the Final Challenge

The host chooses from unplayed categories and the game uses an unused 300-level question. Players see the Final question and current totals.

Step 11

Reveal the winner

The host deliberately controls the final reveal. The Player Display finishes with a clear winning-team celebration.
