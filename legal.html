Privacy policy

Your privacy

Hoot & Holler Hootenanny is designed as a locally operated macOS game. Game play, team selections, scores, and question libraries are handled on the host’s Mac. MediaPro Productions does not receive that game-session information through this website.

This website does not intentionally request player names, create player accounts, sell personal information, or use advertising trackers. Standard technical records may be processed by the services that deliver the website, including GitHub Pages and domain/DNS providers, for security, reliability, and basic operation. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

If you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use it to respond, provide support, and maintain reasonable business records. We do not sell it.

Participant safety

The host is responsible for the room

Barnyard activities are intended for friendly entertainment. The host must use judgment based on the ages, mobility, health, comfort, space, venue rules, and preferences of participants. Any player may decline or modify an activity. The host should skip anything unsuitable and must not pressure participation.

Keep walkways clear, avoid running or unsafe physical activity, and do not use the app as a substitute for supervision or professional safety guidance. Participation is voluntary and undertaken at each participant’s own risk.
